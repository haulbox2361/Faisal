Integrations · Google Account (Admin's own)

The Google OAuth app itself (Client ID / Secret) is registered once in the backend/.env file, not here — a client secret should never sit in a browser. The "Google Account" you connect below is your own (Admin's) sign-in, used the same way each dispatcher connects their own under My Account — nobody shares an inbox.

Filled in automatically once you connect — it's whichever Google account you sign in as. Requires gmail.send, gmail.readonly and drive.file scopes on the same OAuth consent (already set up in googleClient.js).

Connect opens a real Google sign-in popup, handled by the backend (routes/auth.js). If it fails immediately with a Google error page, the backend's .env likely still needs GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID/GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET filled in — see the backend README.
