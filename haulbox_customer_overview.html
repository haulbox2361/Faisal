One Unified System for Your Entire Fleet

Connecting drivers on the road, dispatchers in the office, and business owners on mobile.

Mobile App + Web Portal
📱 1. Mobile App (Drivers & Business Owners)
🚚 Driver Experience Mobile App
  • Simple, Secure Login: Driver logs in using their own unique ID and PIN.
  • Assigned Loads at a Glance: View complete load information, pickup dates, and drop-off instructions.
  • Camera Document Upload: Snap and upload Bills of Lading (BOL) and Proof of Delivery (POD) directly from phone.
  • Multi-Stop Support: Easily upload separate paperwork for each stop on multi-pickup or multi-delivery loads.
  • Smart Quality Check: The app instantly ensures photos are clear, readable, and complete before accepting.
  • Live ETA Updates: Real-time location sharing automatically keeps dispatch and brokers informed.
  • Track Earnings: Drivers can check payment status on completed trips anytime.
💼 Business Owner Experience Dedicated View
  • Dedicated Owner View: Logging into the same mobile app automatically opens a private executive dashboard.
  • Business Snapshot: See revenue, driver pay, estimated profits, active loads, and available drivers at a glance.
  • One-Tap Payments: Review loads ready to pay and mark driver settlements as paid with a single tap.
  • Fleet-Wide Visibility: View all loads and their lifecycle status in real time (read-only for security).
  • Performance Reports: Review daily, weekly, and monthly business summaries on the go.
  • Trend Analytics: Track fleet revenue trends and driver volume with clear visual charts.
💻 2. Web Portal (Dispatchers, Managers & Admins)
🎧 Dispatcher Command Daily Operations
  • Focused Driver Management: Dispatchers see only their own assigned drivers, keeping work organized and clear.
  • Document Verification: Review uploaded driver BOLs and PODs side-by-side with load details.
  • Live Tracking Map: Watch truck positions and accurate estimated arrival times on an easy-to-read map.
  • End-of-Day Driver Notes: Submit a quick daily update for each driver with a friendly 4–5 PM reminder.
🏢 Manager & Admin Hub Full Control
  • Complete Operational Control: Full visibility across all dispatchers, drivers, fleet trucks, and active loads.
  • User Account Management: Quickly create and manage accounts for dispatchers, drivers, and fleet owners.
  • Company-Wide Daily Reports: View daily driver status notes organized by dispatcher to ensure complete daily reporting.
  • End-to-End Oversight: Oversee loads from booking and dispatch through document delivery and final payment.

Core Features & Workflow
📄 3. Automated Document Handling (Zero Data Entry) Hands-Free Automation
  • Instant Rate Confirmation Reading: Upload a Rate Confirmation and key load details (stops, rates, dates) are read automatically.
  • Automatic Multi-Stop Detection: The system identifies all pickup and delivery stops from the document without manual entry.
  • Organized Cloud Storage: Rate Confirmations, BOLs, and PODs are filed automatically into Google Drive folders by load.
  • Instant Broker Emailing: Once a BOL is approved, it is automatically emailed to the broker without manual effort.
  • Automated Spreadsheet Logging: Every load's key details are instantly logged into a Google Sheet for easy record-keeping.
  • Paperless Fleet: Eliminates lost paperwork, manual typing mistakes, and billing delays.
📍 4. Live Tracking & Accurate Arrival Times Live Operations
  • Clean Status Map: View driver locations with simple pins for pickup and drop-off stops—no confusing turn-by-turn clutter.
  • Real GPS Arrival Times: ETAs are calculated using the truck's real GPS position and refreshed automatically.
  • Multi-Stop Progress: Clearly shows the exact next stop in the journey, whether it's pickup #2 or final delivery.
  • Fewer Check Calls: Brokers and dispatchers stay informed with live status, saving time on phone calls.
